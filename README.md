## 🚨 The Problem: "The Three Pillars of Paralysis"

In Malaysia, 1 in 4 adolescents suffers from depression, yet 75% remain untreated. Our research identifies three core barriers:

1. **Stigma Paralysis:** The cultural concept of *jaga maruah* prevents people from seeking help.
2. **Choice Paralysis:** Low mental health literacy leads to confusion about where to find resources.
3. **Crisis Paralysis:** In acute moments, cognitive load prevents users from navigating complex help systems.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Temanak is a **3-in-1 ecosystem** that provides:

* **Anonymous Entry:** Zero-friction access for the 85% of users who are not ready for professional help.
* **AI Companion ("Ruby"):** A hyper-localized, 24/7 empathetic listener that understands Malaysian code-switching (`Rojak`).
* **Institutional Intelligence:** A B2S (Business-to-School) dashboard providing anonymized, aggregated wellness data for proactive intervention.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Anonymous Dashboard:** Mood tracking with 7-day trend analysis.
* **AI Mood Scan:** Privacy-first, on-device facial expression detection to suggest mood logs.
* **Ruby AI Companion:** Voice and text-based empathetic chat with crisis keyword detection.
* **Interactive Self-Help:** 2D/3D rendered breathing and mindfulness exercises.
* **Crisis Support Page:** One-tap connection to local Malaysian hotlines (Befrienders, Talian Kasih).
* **Counsellor Dashboard:** Web-based portal for school administrators to view faculty/student-level wellness trends.

## 🛠 Technical Implementation (Huawei HMS)

Teman utilizes the **Option 1 Model** (AI Element + Huawei Mobile Services).

### **AI Element: HMS ML Kit**

* **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Powers "Ruby" to understand local slang and detect crisis keywords.
* **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR):** Allows users in distress to speak to the app.
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Gives Ruby a calming, human voice for audible support.
* **Face & Body Detection:** Processes facial expressions **on-device** for mood logging without saving images.

### **HMS Core Services**

* **Audio Kit & Graphics Kit:** Powers interactive, high-quality mindfulness modules.
* **hQUIC Kit:** Ensures smooth streaming of resources even on weak rural networks.
* **Account Kit:** Fast, secure one-tap login for partner institutions.
* **Push Kit:** Low-latency notifications for peer support and check-ins.
* **Location Kit:** Optional geolocation to find the nearest physical counseling centers.

## 🏗 System Architecture

Teman follows a **Privacy-by-Design** architecture:

1. **Client Tier:** Native Android application handling on-device AI and UI.
2. **Logic Tier:** Secure API gateway connecting to the Ruby AI (HMS NLP) and the Anonymization Engine.
3. **Data Tier:** Encrypted database (AES-256) storing journal entries and aggregated statistics.
4. **Institutional Tier:** Secure web dashboard for partner analytics.

## 🔒 Data Privacy & PDPA

Teman is 100% compliant with the **Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)**:

* **Explicit Consent:** Granular opt-ins for AI features and data processing.
* **Zero-Storage Camera Policy:** Facial scans are processed in RAM on-device and never saved.
* **Anonymization Engine:** Data provided to institutions is aggregated; individual users are never identifiable.
* **Right to Erasure:** Full "Delete My Account" functionality.
